Whistle In-Browser

Model: Cactus-Compute/whistle. A 17 MB speech recognizer for English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Polish, running on your device. Audio never leaves the browser.

A PyTorch re-implementation of Whistle exported to ONNX (mrfakename/whistle-ONNX), run with ONNX Runtime Web on WebGPU, falling back to WebAssembly. Weights ship as the original 2/4-bit codebook pack (17 MB) and are expanded in the browser. Decoding is greedy; keyword biasing is a simple token-trie boost. Example clips are synthetic speech.
